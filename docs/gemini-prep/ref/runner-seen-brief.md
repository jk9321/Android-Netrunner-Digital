## The job in one paragraph
The Runner AI keeps a private map of Corp cards it has "seen" (`memory`, `src/ai/runnerAI.ts:33`, filled by `remember`, `:85-96`), keyed by instance id and never emptied. The engine is the owner of that fact and follows the rules: an accessed installed card is marked (`runnerKnows`, `src/core/engine/actions/run.ts:462`; rule 7.2.1), a card that changes zone is a new object (1.12.1) and one that goes to HQ, R&D or facedown Archives is no longer tracked (1.12.3; `forgetHiddenCards`, `src/core/engine/helpers.ts:222`), and a reinstalled card stays known only if the Runner saw it go in (7.3.1a; `runnerSawBeforeInstall`, `helpers.ts:210`; `install.ts:178-179`). The map bypasses all of it for installed cards. Count 1 of R0 (`/mnt/project-files/netrunner-ai/runner-rework/r0/count1-audit-map/count-note.md`, part D; ruling beside it) found the classes: `recycled` (a card back through a hidden zone and installed again: 65 changed decisions in 24 of 221 self-play games), `notAccessed` (an access limit), `preAccess` (every candidate of a breach before it is accessed; no rule allows it: 7.4.1a, 7.2.1, 10.2.2b), `breachReplaced` (Burner, Eru Ayase-Pessoa, Beatriz Friere Gonzalez), `installedThisTurn`, and `other` (one Spin Doctor case). The fix: an entry for an installed card exists exactly while the engine marks that card. No new rule, no number.

## The fix (the mechanism is decided; do not choose another)
1. Only `remember` changes. After it runs, for every Corp card installed in a root: the map holds its code if the card is rezzed, face up or `runnerKnows`, and holds no entry otherwise. Its first loop (every root card of a server run successfully this turn) goes.
2. The `memory` parameter and `knownCards()` stay on every reader. No reader is rewritten to read the state. So every caller that passes no map (`evaluate.ts:195`, the Corp's models) is unchanged by construction.
3. **Left exactly as today, on purpose:** an entry whose card is not installed (it sits in HQ, R&D, Archives). Those entries are partly knowledge the rules allow (a known card trashed facedown by an overinstall, 8.5.6a and 4.4.6b; a known card returned to HQ; R&D's contents after a shuffle) and partly a read by instance identity. Sorting them needs an engine fact that does not exist yet (asked of the rules thread). You do not touch them; you size them (arm H below) and name them in the hand-off as a remaining read. The one thing that changes at this boundary: when such a card is installed again without the engine's mark, its entry is gone (rule 1 above).
4. Ice is out: `remember` never took ice. No reading of `knownInHand` or `knownInRd` is added.
5. If you find an installed card the Runner really did see, by the rules, that the engine does not mark, stop and tell me: that is an engine fact for the rules thread.
6. The registry gets the row "what the Runner has seen of the Corp's cards": owner the engine's marks for installed cards; the map as their copy handed to the readers; the hidden-zone entries as a recorded debt with the R-B pointer.
No Corp file is edited. The Corp's play changes all the same, through the reply's Runner (it loses `preAccess`, `notAccessed` and `breachReplaced` inside each sample); that is counted, below.
